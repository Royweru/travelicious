# Wildlife Page Architecture

This document outlines the structural layout and wireframe of the Wildlife Page, serving as an educational and inspirational hub for animal encounters.

## Component Tree

```mermaid
graph TD
    A[Wildlife Page] --> B(Global Navbar)
    
    A --> C(Hero Section)
    C --> C1[Video: Slow-motion cheetah sprint]
    C --> C2[Headline: 'The characters that make every safari unforgettable']
    
    A --> D(The Big Five)
    D --> D1[Grid of Line-drawn Illustrations]
    D --> D2[Species: Lion, Elephant, Buffalo, Leopard, Rhino]
    D --> D3[Success Rate Progress Bars]
    
    A --> E(10x Enhancement: Lesser-Known Species)
    E --> E1[Carousel highlighting 'The Ugly Five' or rare birds]
    
    A --> F(Wildlife Calendar)
    F --> F1[Interactive Matrix: Months vs Events]
    F --> F2[Events: Migration, Calving, Predator Action]
    F --> F3[Clickable cells linking to optimal safaris]
    
    A --> G(10x Enhancement: Conservation Status)
    G --> G1[Global population tracking & awareness]

    A --> H(Global Footer)
```

## Section Details & Enhancements (10x Perspective)
- **Big Five Illustrations**: Using editorial line art rather than stock photos keeps the page sophisticated and avoids the generic "zoo" look.
- **Interactive Calendar**: This is a powerful sales tool. Visualizing *when* things happen helps users decide *when* to book.
- **Added 10x Element**: *Lesser-Known Species*. True luxury is about expertise. Highlighting rare or niche wildlife shows deep local knowledge.
